GEO in 2026: What Gets Your Pages Cited by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity

Generative Engine Optimization explained with 2026 research: what earns AI citations in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and how to measure it.

By the Zorvex Technologies team

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the work of making your content visible in AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode. You’ll also hear it called AEO (answer engine optimisation), LLMO or simply AI SEO. The term comes from a 2023 research paper, and in 2026 it has become a real part of every serious SEO plan.

What the 2026 research says

The best paragraph matters most

A study published in October 2026 looked at 158,099 AI answers with 468,037 citations across commercial questions. One consistent finding: pages with a strong, directly relevant paragraph got cited more on every engine tested. The lift was about 9.5% on ChatGPT, 7.8% on Gemini and 4.8% on Perplexity.

Engines also behave differently. A title that matches the question went with more citations from Gemini (about +17%) and Perplexity (about +5%), but made almost no difference on ChatGPT. One page can’t be tuned for “AI” as a single thing.

A small share of pages wins most citations

Peec AI analysed 30 million sources cited across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews. On ChatGPT, the 31% of URLs cited most often took 59% of all citations. List-style pages performed especially well: 52% of listicles on ChatGPT reached a high citation rate, even though they were under a fifth of cited URLs.

Be careful with “share of citations” dashboards

A 2026 research survey found that in one setup, 57.8% of repeated ChatGPT prompts didn’t run a web search at all. If a tool only counts answers that contain citations, it can overstate your visibility. Its authors say visibility should be measured across several engines, prompts and time periods, not as a single “cited by ChatGPT” score.

New ways to see AI traffic

OpenAI opened its Business Hub in beta on 10 November 2026. Verified businesses can track ChatGPT mentions and click-throughs. Meanwhile, one study found 79.2% of 28,500 sites got at least one AI referral in September 2026, but for most of them AI visits were still small next to organic search.

A practical GEO checklist

  • Lead each section with a clear answer in 2–3 sentences, then add detail.
  • Match the question in your headings (“How much does a sport court cost?”), which helps on Gemini and Perplexity.
  • Publish comparison and “best of” content where it genuinely helps buyers, with honest pros and cons.
  • Add original facts: prices, timelines, data from your own projects. Unique facts are worth citing.
  • Keep ranking in Google. AI Mode and AI Overviews start from top search results.
  • Track several engines and many prompts over time, and claim your ChatGPT Business Hub listing.
