Ads in Google AI Mode: Multiturn Ads, Up to Four Ads and 10 New Countries Including India

Google now shows ads on AI Mode follow-up questions and has expanded AI Mode ads to India, Australia and more. Here’s how to get your campaigns ready.

By the Zorvex Technologies team

AI Mode is Google's chat-style search, and it's getting more ads. Three changes from the last month are worth knowing about if you run Google Ads.

1. Multiturn ads: ads on follow-up questions

On 28 October 2026 Google announced multiturn ads in the US. They let ads appear on the follow-up questions people ask during an AI Mode conversation, not just the first search. Search Engine Roundtable confirmed they were live in early November, with “Sponsored” ads showing on the third and fourth follow-ups too.

Follow-up questions tend to be the most specific and the closest to buying (“which one is cheapest?”, “who installs this near me?”). That makes these ad spots valuable.

The good news: you don’t need a new campaign type. Google uses your existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. Exact and phrase match can show on the first question; broad match, AI Max, Shopping and Performance Max can show on the first question and the follow-ups.

2. Up to four ads per AI Mode answer

By early November, AI Mode was showing up to four ads on a single response. Google says click-through rates are already similar between sponsored and organic product listings inside AI Mode.

3. AI Mode ads expand to 10 more countries

Also on 28 October, Google expanded AI Mode ads to Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore. Google says AI Mode queries are on average three times longer than traditional searches.

Multiturn ads are still US-only for now, and Google hasn’t said when they’ll reach other countries.

The catch: limited reporting

The downside is reporting. AI Mode results are mixed into your normal campaign and search terms reports, and right now there’s no way to tell a click on a follow-up question from a click on the first one.

How to get ready

  • Test broad match or AI Max on proven campaigns, with strong negative keyword lists.
  • Write assets that answer comparison and pricing questions, since these match follow-up queries.
  • Clean up your Merchant Center feed with detailed titles and attributes for Shopping and PMax.
  • Review search terms weekly, because longer, conversational queries will appear.
  • Use value-based bidding so Google bids more for the leads and sales that matter most.
