Google Ads Updates, November 2026: AI Max Tracking, 7-Day Smart Bidding Learning and More

The Google Ads changes that matter this month: AI Max click tracking, the 7-day Smart Bidding learning limit, PMax income exclusions, purchase badges and more.

By the Zorvex Technologies team

Google Ads shipped a long list of changes this autumn. Here are the ones we think matter most for small and mid-sized advertisers, and what to do about each.

1. You can now identify AI Max traffic

AI Max can show your Search ads on queries beyond your keyword list. Google has now documented a ValueTrack parameter, {search_term_matchtype}, that helps separate normal keyword clicks, AI Max broad expansions and keywordless matches in your click data.

Do this: add it to your tracking template so you can compare AI Max traffic with your keyword traffic in GA4 or your CRM.

2. Smart Bidding “Learning” now lasts 7 days at most

Google rewrote its help page as About Smart Bidding learning. It now says the Learning status never lasts more than 7 days, down from the 7–14 days often quoted before. Performance Max and Demand Gen campaigns may need up to 7 days to ramp up serving.

Do this: avoid several target changes inside one conversion cycle, and judge performance after the ramp-up, not during it.

3. Household income exclusions in Performance Max

Performance Max campaigns now support campaign-level household income exclusions across all placements, a control advertisers have asked for.

4. Attributed Branded Searches

Launched on 3 November, this free measurement tool shows how many people searched for your brand on Google or YouTube after seeing your Demand Gen or YouTube video ads. Find it under Goals > Summary > Consideration.

5. Purchase volume badges on Shopping ads

Eligible Shopping ads can now automatically show recent sales figures to build trust, as long as you report item-level conversions.

6. Merchant Center “Ads opportunities” for agencies

Agencies can now spot products with no ad coverage, or very little traffic, across several Merchant Center accounts from one dashboard.

7. Invalid traffic overview card

A new card in Google Ads shows how much you were credited back for invalid (fake) clicks.

8. Google backs dynamic conversion values

Google Ads Liaison Ginny Marvin said feeding dynamic, predicted conversion values into Smart Bidding is a core part of value-based bidding, not a workaround. If some leads are worth far more than others, pass that value to Google.

Our top three actions for December

  • Add AI Max tracking and review its search terms weekly.
  • Set up conversion values (even estimated ones) so Smart Bidding chases profit, not just volume.
  • Check your Attributed Branded Searches if you run YouTube or Demand Gen.
